TITLE
A quadratic critical-value conjecture for the fifth Bessel moment

AUTHOR
Jonas Matuzas

VERSION
4 -- 16 September 2026

ABSTRACT (LaTeX)
We conjecture a quadratic critical-value evaluation of
$\int_0^\infty K_0(t)^5\,\dd t$ for a weight-three, level-60 newform.
We prove that its twisted symmetric-square value at $2$ equals
$\sqrt{15}\pi^2$ times its Petersson norm, and give exact norm and
coefficient-twist identities. These modular theorems identify the companion
norm formula with Lim--Tu--Yu's conjecture; they do not prove the Bessel
comparison. Chuang's period formulas relate the even and odd determinants.
Directed intervals bound the individual-period discrepancy by $10^{-358}$.
Full proofs and reproducible certificates accompany the note.

COMMENTS
4 pages. Complete ancillary proofs, exact modular certificates and directed-interval verification programs included. Version 4 proves the purely modular equation (3.5); the Bessel conjectures remain open.
